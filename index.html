How it works

Your wardrobe, in 3D, in three steps.

1

Build your model

Scan your body with your phone, or just enter your sizes.

2

Scan your clothes

Hold a piece in front of the camera and turn it around. It joins your wardrobe in 3D.

3

Try on outfits

Mix your pieces on your 3D model and look at them from every side.

What you can do

Get dressed and shop with confidence.

Try before you buy

See it with what you own

Check what a new piece goes with in your wardrobe before you buy it.

Never forget a piece

All your clothes in one place, sorted by category and season, like your real wardrobe.

Build outfits in seconds

Pick a top, bottoms and shoes, and see the whole outfit on you without changing clothes.

Real scans, not AI-generated pictures

What you see is built from scans of your body and your clothes, so it stays true to the real thing.

Your scans stay yours
